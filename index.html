02   Sight the top

Hold the phone on its side at eye level, with the screen facing sideways. Sight along a long edge toward the top end of the phone (the earpiece end). Raise that end toward the treetop. Keep the phone’s screen plane vertical.

How your two triangles work

The little triangle behind you has your eye height as its vertical side. Its horizontal side is eye height ÷ tan(angle).

Add that little distance to the distance you walked. The big triangle’s height is that total distance × tan(angle).

This simplifies to height = eye height + distance × tan(angle).

Results are estimates. Uneven ground, irregular steps, leaning trees and small aiming errors affect accuracy. Stand far enough back to keep the angle under 60° where possible.
